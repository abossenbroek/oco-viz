# Gallery Context — Contemporary Art Reference Lenses

Not a database — comparative positioning tools for contextualizing oco-viz output against contemporary art practice. Used by the art-director agent to assess where the work sits in the current landscape.

---

## Reference Artists

### Refik Anadol — The Anti-Reference

Maximalist data art. Turbulent flowing forms projected on building facades. Spectacle-first, sensation-first. Beautiful, immersive, and ultimately decorative.

**oco-viz is the deliberate anti-Anadol.** Where Anadol maximizes beauty and spectacle, Soot minimizes it. Where Anadol uses color and motion to seduce, Soot uses achromatic stillness to confront.

**Flag any drift toward Anadol.** If the image feels spectacular, flowing, beautiful, immersive in a pleasant way — it has drifted. Soot is not data art. It is data confrontation.

### William Kentridge — Material Complicity

Charcoal on black. South African mining/industrial history. The material (charcoal, coal dust) is complicit in the subject matter — drawing with the residue of the very industry being depicted.

**Soot should carry the same material complicity.** CO2 is rendered as its own physical residue — soot, the byproduct of the combustion that produced the CO2. The rendering material IS the subject. This is not arbitrary aesthetic choice; it is conceptual necessity.

**Evaluate for:** material conviction, industrial weight, the sense that the substance itself carries history and culpability.

### Ryoji Ikeda — Data Minimalism

Pure black and white. Extreme asymmetry. Vast fields of structured data rendered as stark visual pattern. The void is not empty — it is a positive element.

**Void treatment should aspire to this severity.** The black in a Soot image should have the same active, intentional quality as the black in an Ikeda installation. It is not background; it is the other half of the composition.

**Evaluate for:** void authority, compositional severity, the discipline of extreme reduction.

### Trevor Paglen — Invisible Infrastructure

Making the invisible visible. Surveillance systems, undersea cables, classified satellites — things that shape our world but remain unseen.

**CO2 is normally invisible.** Rendering it as physical substance is an act of revelation in Paglen's tradition. The viewer sees what is normally hidden, and the seeing itself is the confrontation.

**Evaluate for:** the sensation of revelation — does viewing the image make the invisible feel newly, uncomfortably present?

### Olafur Eliasson — Eco-Critical Persuasion

Environmental installations that use beauty as persuasion. Ice blocks melting in public squares. Artificial suns in museum halls. Beautiful, accessible, affecting.

**Soot rejects beauty-as-persuasion.** Where Eliasson uses beauty to make ecological crisis approachable, Soot uses dread to make it inescapable. This is not gentle awareness-raising; it is confrontation with the physical residue of combustion.

**Flag any drift toward Eliasson.** If the image feels beautiful, hopeful, or accessible, it has softened. Soot does not seduce; it chokes.

## Gallery Projection Context

The intended presentation format is large-scale projection in dark rooms:

- **Scale**: Projected at 4K minimum, potentially across entire walls
- **Environment**: Dark room — the void IS the room. Frame edges must be absolute black to blend with walls.
- **Light source**: The plume is the only light in the room. It illuminates the viewers' faces.
- **Viewing distance**: Both close (texture detail) and far (compositional impact) must work.

### Implications for Review

- **Banding and noise** are painfully visible at gallery scale — what looks fine at 1080p is unacceptable at 4K projection
- **Frame edges** must be absolute black (`#000000`) — any grey will break the illusion of the void being the room
- **Bloom and glow** artifacts become enormous at scale — what reads as subtle on a monitor becomes a glowing halo projected 3 meters wide
- **Temporal artifacts** (flicker, popping) are nauseating at gallery scale and viewing duration

## Positioning Assessment Template

When positioning a reviewed image:

1. **Anadol distance**: How far from decorative spectacle? (further = better)
2. **Kentridge weight**: Does the material carry history and complicity?
3. **Ikeda severity**: Does the composition achieve minimalist authority?
4. **Paglen revelation**: Does the invisible feel newly, uncomfortably present?
5. **Eliasson distance**: How far from beauty-as-persuasion? (further = better)

Synthesize into 2-4 sentences that contextualize where this specific image sits relative to these reference points.
