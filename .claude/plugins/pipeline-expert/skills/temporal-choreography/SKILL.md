# Temporal Choreography — Long-Form Animation Pacing

Long-form narrative logic for continuous volumetric presence. No edit cuts — continuous evolution with phase-based state machine. The substance emerges, confronts, and recedes but never fully departs. Something always remains.

---

## State Machine Phases

| Phase | Duration | Character | Turbulence | Emission | Dissolution | Camera |
|-------|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Quiescent** | 20-30% | Stillness before. Barely visible traces in void. Tension of absence. The room is dark; something is forming. | 0.0-0.1 | 0.0-0.05 | 0.0 | Static or Tarkovsky Meditation |
| **Building** | 15-25% | Emergence. Substance coalesces from darkness. Growing dread. Density increases, turbulence awakens. | 0.1-0.4 | 0.05-0.4 | 0.0-0.1 | Slow push-in (Herzog Approach) |
| **Chaotic** | 30-40% | Full presence. Dense, turbulent, overwhelming. The confrontation. The volume at its most oppressive — the viewer cannot escape the weight. | 0.4-1.0 | 0.4-1.0 | 0.1-0.3 | Close, engulfed (Ultra-Wide or Normal) |
| **Aftermath** | 15-25% | Dissolution. The substance dissipates but never fully vanishes. Something remains. The air is never clean again. | 1.0-0.2 | 1.0-0.1 | 0.3-0.8 | Slow withdrawal (Static Witness) |

Phase durations are approximate and sum to 100%. The Chaotic phase is always the longest — the confrontation should not be brief.

---

## Phase Transitions

- Transitions are gradual: 10-15% of total duration as overlap between phases
- No hard cuts between phases — the substance evolves, it does not switch
- Turbulence, emission, and dissolution have independent ease curves
- **Building -> Chaotic** has the steepest ramp (acceleration of dread, the moment the substance overwhelms)
- **Chaotic -> Aftermath** is the slowest transition (the substance resists departure, it clings)
- **Aftermath -> Quiescent** (if looping): the traces never fully vanish. The loop restarts with residue.

## Conductor Controls (Keyframeable)

| Control | Range | Maps To |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Turbulence Intensity | 0.0-1.0 | Noise amplitude, octave activation count (0.0 = laminar, 1.0 = full turbulent cascade) |
| Emission Rate | 0.0-1.0 | Source emission strength, particle spawn rate, density injection |
| Dissolution Speed | 0.0-1.0 | Edge dissolution radius, particle drift velocity, boundary erosion |
| Temporal Compression | 0.1-10.0 | Playback speed multiplier (0.3 = exhibition glacial, 1.0 = study, 2.0+ = sketch) |
| Breath Cycle | 0.0-1.0 | Periodic contraction/expansion amplitude — the volume inhales and exhales |

The Breath Cycle is the most important subtle control. It gives the substance the uncanny quality of a living, choking presence — a slow rhythmic pulse that is felt before it is seen.

---

## Easing Functions

| Context | Function | Character | Tempo Multiplier |
|---------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Exhibition** | heavy_ease_in | Glacial start, building weight. Time itself feels heavy. The viewer adjusts to the pace and then the pace owns them. | 0.3 |
| **Study** | smoothstep | Standard S-curve. Comfortable for iterative review and parameter tuning. | 1.0 |
| **Sketch** | linear | Direct, no pacing. Fast preview for structural decisions. | 2.0+ |

Exhibition easing is non-negotiable. The glacial pace IS the aesthetic. If it feels slow, the viewer has not surrendered to the piece.

---

## Breath Mechanics

The volume breathes — a subtle periodic contraction and expansion that operates beneath conscious perception.

| Parameter | Exhibition | Study | Sketch |
|-----------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Period | 8-12 seconds | 4-6 seconds | N/A |
| Amplitude | 2-5% of volume radius | 3-8% | N/A |
| Waveform | Asymmetric: slow inhale (60%), sharp exhale (40%) | Sinusoidal | N/A |
| Phase offset | Random per octave (turbulent layers breathe independently) | Uniform | N/A |

The asymmetric waveform is critical: the slow inhale builds tension, the sharper exhale releases it. This mirrors organic breathing under stress — labored, uneven, oppressive.

---

## Loop Strategy

For continuous gallery exhibition, the piece loops. But not seamlessly:

- **Aftermath never fully resolves** — traces remain when Quiescent restarts
- **Each cycle accumulates residue** — after 3-4 loops, the background carries persistent wisps
- **The loop point is imperceptible** — no fade to black, no reset. The substance is always present in some form.
- **Loop duration**: 8-15 minutes for exhibition (viewer may enter/exit at any point)

## Temporal Validation Checklist

1. Do phase durations sum to approximately 100%?
2. Is the Chaotic phase the longest (30-40%)?
3. Are transitions gradual (10-15% overlap)?
4. Is Building -> Chaotic the steepest ramp?
5. Is Chaotic -> Aftermath the slowest transition?
6. Does the Breath Cycle operate at exhibition pace (8-12s period)?
7. Is temporal compression set to 0.3 for exhibition?
8. Does the loop accumulate residue (never fully clean)?
9. Is there emotional justification for every tempo change?
