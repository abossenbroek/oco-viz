# Virtual Cinematographer — Camera, Composition & Motion

Camera craft for volumetric substance. Every position is a statement. Every move is motivated by emotional intent — never by information delivery. The camera is a witness, not a tour guide.

---

## Compositional Rules

- **Rule of Asymmetry**: Mass off-center. Void as counterweight. Never centered — symmetry is comfort, comfort is failure.
- **Vertical Emphasis**: Rising emissions, industrial stacks, ascending dread. Camera slightly below looking up. The substance looms.
- **Frame Fill**: 60-80% for exhibition. The substance must dominate, but the void must breathe. Below 60% feels incidental; above 80% suffocates without purpose.
- **Negative Space**: The void is active, not empty. It defines the substance by its absence. Aspire to Ikeda severity — the black is a positive compositional element.
- **Edge Discipline**: Frame edges absolute black. No content touches edges. The projection dissolves into the room. Content within 5% of frame edge is a defect.
- **Depth Layering**: Multiple density layers must be visible — foreground detail, mid-ground mass, background traces. The volume has depth, not just surface.

## Lens Packages

| Lens | Focal Equiv. | Aspect | Character | When to Use |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Anamorphic Wide** | 28-40mm | 2.39:1 | Oppressive horizontal compression, horizontal streak in highlights, breathing distortion at edges | Industrial pall, wide plumes, suffocating coverage. The substance stretches beyond escape. |
| **Telephoto Compressed** | 85-135mm | 16:9 | Flattened perspective, layered depth planes, claustrophobic stacking | Dense core detail, geological layering. Distance collapses; the substance is inescapable. |
| **Normal Witness** | 50mm | 16:9 | Documentary neutrality, human-eye perspective, no distortion agenda | Default. The viewer as witness. Honest, unflinching, complicit through observation. |
| **Ultra-Wide Immersive** | 18-24mm | 2.39:1 | Engulfing perspective, viewer inside the volume, barrel distortion at extremes | Mathematical sublime, scale overwhelming. The viewer is not outside the substance — they are within it. |

**Lens selection is not technical — it is emotional.** The question is not "what shows the data best" but "what makes the viewer feel most implicated."

## Motivated Motion Languages

| Language | Character | Speed | Reference |
|----------|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Herzog Approach** | Slow push-in, inevitable, unflinching. The camera approaches what it dreads. It does not want to get closer, but it must. | 0.05-0.15 m/s | Werner Herzog, *Lessons of Darkness* — the oil field approach shots |
| **Ikeda Mathematical** | Precise lateral tracking, geometric, measured. Data-driven movement with mechanical precision. | 0.1-0.3 m/s | Ryoji Ikeda, *test pattern* — the scanning precision of structured data |
| **Tarkovsky Meditation** | Near-static, glacial drift, time dissolves. The camera barely moves; the substance evolves around it. | 0.01-0.05 m/s | Andrei Tarkovsky, *Stalker* — zone sequences where time itself thickens |
| **Kentridge Trace** | Following the path of mark-making, tracing the edge where substance meets void. | 0.08-0.2 m/s | William Kentridge, *Shadow Procession* — the line between presence and absence |
| **Static Witness** | Locked off. No movement. The substance moves; the camera does not. Complicity through observation. | 0 m/s | Surveillance, CCTV — the camera that records without intervening |

**Forbidden motions:** Orbital/turntable, zoom-to-detail, fly-through, dollying out (retreating from the substance). These are presentation gestures. This is not a presentation.

## Camera Height & Angle

| Position | Angle | Effect |
|----------|-------|--------|
| **Below (0.3-0.7 of volume height)** | Looking up 5-15 degrees | The substance looms. Industrial chimneys, rising emissions. Dread of scale. |
| **Level (0.5 of volume height)** | Horizontal | Documentary witness. Eye-level confrontation. |
| **Slightly Above (0.6-0.8 of volume height)** | Looking down 5-10 degrees | Geological survey. Sedimentary layers. The weight of accumulation. |

Never fully above (bird's-eye) — that is the perspective of control, of mastery. The viewer should never feel they command the substance.

---

## Depth of Field

Exhibition tier uses shallow depth to isolate density layers:
- **Focus plane**: Set at the densest region of the volume
- **Bokeh character**: Soft, not sharp-edged. The out-of-focus substance dissolves, not blurs.
- **Rack focus**: Extremely rare. When used, it shifts attention between foreground wisp and background mass — the substance is everywhere.

---

## Composition Validation Checklist

1. Is the mass off-center? (Symmetry = failure)
2. Is the void active, not dead? (Black is a positive element)
3. Are frame edges absolute black? (No content within 5% of edge)
4. Is frame fill 60-80%? (Too little = incidental; too much = suffocating)
5. Are multiple depth layers visible? (Not flat)
6. Is the camera height emotionally motivated? (Not arbitrary)
7. Does the lens choice serve the emotional register? (Not informational)
8. Is every camera move motivated? (No arbitrary animation)
