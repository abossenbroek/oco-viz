# The Sublime -- Monumentality Gate

Kantian sublime: awe combined with dread. The volume must overwhelm the
viewer's capacity to comprehend its scale. Not beauty -- confrontation.

---

## Criteria

| Criterion | Threshold | Evidence Required |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Scale overwhelming | Viewer cannot encompass entire volume in perception | Camera position analysis (volume exceeds comfortable framing) |
| Dread presence | Unease, not comfort | Artistic verdict -- mood test against Soot references |
| Geological weight | Sedimentary layering or folding visible | Visual inspection of anisotropic Z-squash patterns |
| Inevitability | Substance feels unstoppable, inexorable | Motion analysis -- heavy settling, no retreat |
| Confrontation | Viewer implicated in emission narrative | Artistic verdict -- complicity not observation |
| Void quality | Active negative space (void presses back) | Black regions feel present, not empty |
| Material reference | Identifiable physical analog (coal, ash, carbon) | Subjective material conviction test |

---

## Assessment

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| PASS | Volume overwhelms perception. Dread present without melodrama. Geological weight convincing. Viewer feels implicated in industrial emission. Void is active, not dead. |
| CONCERN | Scale impressive but comprehensible. Some dread but comfort creeps in. Geological quality present but not dominant. Confrontation partial -- viewer can maintain safe distance. |
| FAIL | Volume feels manageable, decorative, or comfortable. No dread. No geological weight. Viewer observes without implication. Pretty rather than confrontational. |

Primary: Auteur
Secondary: Sculptor (material weight), Choreographer (temporal inevitability)
